Why this roadmap?

Learn in the right order.

Each stage builds on the previous one: fundamentals create confidence, DSA sharpens problem solving, projects prove practical ability, and interview prep converts preparation into job readiness.

🧱

Build Fundamentals

Understand computers, programming basics, Linux, networking, and professional communication.

🧠

Master Problem Solving

Learn programming, DSA, algorithms, OOP, and disciplined coding practice.

🚀

Build Real Projects

Create applications, document them on GitHub, and grow a portfolio that shows what you can build.

🎯

Become Job Ready

Prepare for coding rounds, technical interviews, HR rounds, resumes, internships, and applications.
